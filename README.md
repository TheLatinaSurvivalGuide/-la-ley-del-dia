# 📜 La Ley del Dia
### From "The Latina Survival Guide" by Charo Toledo

**La Ley del Dia** is a daily wisdom Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to empower Latina women through 55 Survival Laws. This app serves as a daily ritual for reflection, journaling, and sharing the "telenovela logic" that keeps us resilient.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Daily Law Rotation**: A new survival law every day, automatically updated.
- **Bilingual Experience**: All laws, reflections, and prompts in both English and Spanish.
- **Personal Journal**: Save your daily reflections locally on your device.
- **Streak Counter**: Track your consistency with a "🔥" badge.
- **Shareable Cards**: Generate branded gold cards of each law for Instagram.

---

## 📱 How to Install on Your iPhone
1. Open the live link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) at the bottom.
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen."**
4. Tap **Add** in the top right corner.

---

## 🎨 Branding & Design
- **Colors**: Inspired by the book cover's vintage, cinematic aesthetic (#D33F2A Red, #F5E6D3 Cream).
- **Typography**: Elegant serif for laws, clean sans-serif for UI.
- **Concept**: A daily ritual, not a productivity app.
